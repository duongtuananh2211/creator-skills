# Storytelling for Short-Form Vertical Video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) for Personal-Brand Creators

Research date: 2026-10-29. Tags: [OFFICIAL] platform-stated · [STUDY] research/data (incl. platform internal data) · [PRACTITIONER] creator/vendor advice · [BG] background knowledge, unverified in this session.
Scope note: ~19 tool calls; several primary pages (TikTok Creator Academy, YouTube Help Center Shorts pages, Mosseri's original Jan-2025 video) were not retrieved directly. Those claims are reached through secondary coverage and flagged.

## 1. What the platforms officially say

### Takeaway
All three platforms reward the same things: people stop scrolling, keep watching, and share. TikTok's official ad guidance is the most specific on craft: hook within 6 seconds, core message within 3 seconds, text at 5–10 words/sec, 9:16, an unpolished creator style, and a closing CTA. Instagram officially names reshare, full watch, like, and audio-page visits as its key Reels predictions, and down-ranks reposted or watermarked content. For Shorts, YouTube's main official statement is a metrics one: since 31 Mar 2025 a view counts on any start or replay, and "engaged views" or "viewed vs. swiped away" are what actually show retention.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok**
- [OFFICIAL] "Prioritize your hook in the first 6 seconds" to boost engagement and watch time; "Introduce your content proposition in the first 3 seconds" for better recall and awareness. Source: [TikTok Ads Help: Creative best practices for performance ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [OFFICIAL] "We recommend displaying 5-10 words per second when using text." The same page recommends captions or overlays for context. Source: [TikTok Ads Help: Creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [OFFICIAL] Recommended structure: "Have a hook, highlight your unique selling points, and end with a clear call-to-action." It also advises a strong CTA that steers viewers toward "a specific thought, feeling, or action." Source: [TikTok Ads Help: Creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [OFFICIAL] "We recommend featuring people such as creators, employees, or customers"; favor a "DIY or not overly polished style"; "Lean into trends"; orient vertically at 9:16 and shoot at least 720p; keep content inside the UI safe zone. The page does not state an ideal video length. Source: [TikTok Ads Help: Creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [OFFICIAL/STUDY, dated Oct 2020, possibly outdated] TikTok's "9 creative tips" (auction ads):
  - "Over 63% of the videos with the highest CTR highlight their key message or product within the first 3 seconds."
  - Almost half of the best-performing auction ads told their story in an emotionally appealing way, usually relatable, lighthearted, and positive.
  - "Break the fourth wall": 1 in 3 of the highest-VTR ads address the viewer directly.
  - 40% of the highest-VTR ads used text overlays, effects, or stickers.
  - Vertical videos average a 25% higher 6-second watch-through rate.
  - Tracks above 120 BPM often drive higher VTR.
  - 61% of the best-performing ads used half or more of the tips.

  All of these are correlational and ad-focused. Source: [TikTok for Business: 9 creative tips to drive auction ad performance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)
- [OFFICIAL] TikTok Next 2026 trend forecast (Jan 2026) is themed "Irreplaceable Instinct" and has three signals: "Reali-TEA" (authenticity, real stories, humour, emotion over escapism), "Curiosity Detours," and "Emotional ROI." Sources: [TikTok Newsroom: Introducing TikTok Next 2026](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-tiktok-next-2026-our-trend-forecast-for-marketers-for-the-year-ahead?lang=en); [TikTok Next 2026 Trend Report PDF](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_Next_2026_Trend_Report.pdf)

**Instagram**
- [OFFICIAL, May 2023, possibly partly outdated] Mosseri, "Instagram Ranking Explained": for Reels, the most important predictions are how likely you are to "reshare a reel, watch a reel all the way through, like it, and go to the audio page" (the audio-page visit is a proxy for being inspired to make your own reel). Reels that are "low-resolution or watermarked," muted, bordered, mostly text, or "already been posted on Instagram" are made less visible. Source: [Instagram Blog: Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL via secondary] January 2025: Mosseri named watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach as the three most important signals. Likes are "slightly more important" for follower (connected) reach, and sends for non-follower (unconnected) reach. Buffer points out that the "sends are 3–5x likes" multiplier repeated by many blogs has no primary source. Sources: [Buffer: How the Instagram Algorithm Works](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/); [Kompozy: Mosseri restates signals](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)
- [OFFICIAL via secondary] Mosseri: "We want to create a platform that gives more distribution to the original content creator than to those who repost it." Source: [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/). The April 2022 originality-ranking change was confirmed to TechCrunch. Source: [Tubefilter, 21 Apr 2022](https://www.tubefilter.com/2022/04/21/instagram-adam-mosseri-algorithm-change-original-content/)
- [PRACTITIONER/news, unverified] Secondary blogs report an April 2026 expansion of originality enforcement to photos and carousels. Accounts that mostly repost would lose recommendation to non-followers, with a reported ~30-day evaluation window and transformative edits counting as original. I found no primary Instagram post for this. Source: [k24.digital](https://k24.digital/entertainment/original-content-era-what-instagrams-new-update-means-for-content-creators/amp)
- [PRACTITIONER, unverified] Buffer paraphrases Mosseri telling Colin & Samir that about half of videos are watched without sound. Source: [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)

**YouTube Shorts**
- [OFFICIAL via secondary press] From 31 Mar 2025, Shorts views "count the number of times your Short starts to play or replay with no minimum watch time requirement." The older metric survives as "engaged views" in Analytics Advanced Mode, and monetization and YPP still run on engaged views. Sources: [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404562/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views.html); [MobileSyrup, 27 Mar 2025](https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/03/27/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views/)
- [OFFICIAL metric via secondary] Shorts analytics include "Viewed vs. swiped away," whose positive side is now labelled "Stayed to watch." Neither label means the viewer finished the Short. YouTube publishes no universal benchmark, and creators are told to compare against their own Shorts of similar length and format. Source: [ContentStudio: YouTube Shorts analytics guide](https://contentstudio.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide)

### Inferences
- The three platforms share one ranking target: a scroll-stop, then watch time or completion, then a share/send. For a personal-brand storyteller, that means a script needs (a) a frame-1 reason to stop, (b) an open loop that pays off late, and (c) a "send-to-a-friend" moment, meaning a line or insight relatable enough that someone forwards it.
- Instagram treats audio-page visits as a positive signal, so reusable or trend audio has some ranking value there. Watermarks from other apps are an explicit negative, so cross-post clean exports.
- TikTok's 5–10 words/sec figure is a maximum reading speed for overlay text, not a speaking rate. On-screen text should stay well under that (see section 4).
- Most hard numbers are from ads (TikTok 2020 auction-ad study). Applying them to organic personal-brand content is plausible, but it's an extrapolation.

### Gaps
- Not retrieved: TikTok Creator Academy / Creator Center organic creative guidance, YouTube Help Center Shorts best-practice pages, Creator Insider videos, and Mosseri's original Jan-2025 video. All Mosseri 2025 wording here is via secondary sources.
- No official current ideal length from any platform in the sources retrieved. Practitioner claims such as "Instagram does not recommend Reels >3 min to non-followers" appeared in one blog only and are unverified.
- [BG] TikTok's 2020 newsroom post "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou" listed signals such as whether a user finishes a video. It was not re-fetched here (https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you).

## 2. Hooks in the first 1–3 seconds, retention curves, and loops

### Takeaway
The only hook evidence that comes from a platform is TikTok's: key message within 3 seconds, hook within 6, direct address, and text overlays all correlate with better ad CTR/VTR. Every percentage for specific hook types (pattern interrupt, result-first, and so on) comes from vendors and is unverified. YouTube's Shorts metrics split the problem cleanly: the hook decides "stayed to watch," and pacing and payoff decide average % viewed.

### Cited Findings
- [STUDY, platform internal, 2020] Over 63% of TikTok's highest-CTR videos put the key message or product in the first 3 seconds. Source: [TikTok 9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)
- [STUDY, platform internal, 2020] Direct address ("break the fourth wall") appears in 1 in 3 of the highest-VTR ads, and text overlays in 40%. Source: [TikTok 9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)
- [PRACTITIONER/vendor] OpusClip says 50–60% of viewers who drop off do so in the first three seconds (no methodology given). Source: [OpusClip: Ideal YouTube Shorts length & format](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention)
- [PRACTITIONER/vendor, unverified] An OpusClip analysis of 34,635 TikToks, cited by Lemonlight, reports that ~90% of underperforming videos fail in the first 3 seconds. Source: [Lemonlight](https://www.lemonlight.com/the-top-10-hooks-businesses-can-use-to-make-tiktok-marketing-work/)
- [PRACTITIONER, unverified] A widely repeated "Meta internal" figure says 65% of people who watch the first 3 seconds watch ≥10 seconds, and 45% watch 30 seconds. No primary Meta publication was found. Source (secondary): [Grow-it: Short-form video retention benchmarks](https://www.grow-it.se/en/resources/short-form-video-retention-benchmarks)
- [PRACTITIONER, unverified] One agency blog reports 47 A/B tests in which pattern-interrupt openings averaged a +85% retention lift. Not peer-reviewed. Source: [Grow-it](https://www.grow-it.se/en/resources/short-form-video-retention-benchmarks)
- [PRACTITIONER, unverified] "71% of TikTok users decide whether to keep watching in the first three seconds" is cited without a named study. Source: [Teleprompter.com: TikTok 3-second rule](https://teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-3-second-rule)
- [PRACTITIONER] Diagnostic rule: a low viewed-vs-swiped-away rate means the hook or topic didn't stop the scroll. A high viewed rate with low average % viewed points at pacing, build-up, and payoff. A Short can have a weak stayed-to-watch rate and a strong average % viewed at the same time. Sources: [Subscribr: Shorts analytics](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-growth); [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide)
- [PRACTITIONER, heuristic] Targets of ≥75–80% stayed-to-watch and ~80%+ average % viewed come from third-party blogs. There is no official threshold. Source: [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide)
- [PRACTITIONER] OpusClip's bold-claim hook: make a strong statement in the first ~2 seconds and use the next beat for proof or credibility. OpusClip notes that hard-claim hooks can feel off-tone in storytelling videos. Source: [OpusClip: TikTok hook formulas](https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-hook-formulas)
- [STUDY, limited relevance] The only academic item surfaced was a CHI 2024 attention study (n=41). It measured sustained attention, not openings. Source: noted in search results; see [ResearchGate: Impact of Short Form Content on Consumer Attention](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390814911_The_Impact_of_Short_Form_Content_on_Consumer_Attention_and_Marketing_ROI) (quality not assessed)

### Inferences
- Hook taxonomy for the skill: all types below are [BG]/[PRACTITIONER] folklore with no controlled public evidence for one beating another.
  1. Visual hook: unusual frame or motion in frame 1.
  2. Text-on-screen hook: a headline overlay, needed because many people watch muted.
  3. Verbal hook: the first spoken line.
  4. Pattern interrupt: a cut, zoom, sound, or object.
  5. In medias res: start at the peak moment of the story.
  6. Result-first / transformation reveal.
  7. Direct question or "you" address.
  8. Contrarian or bold claim.
  9. Curiosity gap / open loop ("the 3rd one cost me my job").
- The best-supported advice is to stack visual, text, and verbal hooks in the first ~1–3 seconds and say the topic or payoff promise early. That matches TikTok's 3-second key-message finding.
- Retention-curve shapes [BG]: a steep initial drop is normal. A flat "plateau" after the hook is the goal. Mid-video cliffs mark slow beats. A curve that rises above 100% at the end, or a bump, signals rewatches or loops. Loop endings, where the last line flows into the first, raise replays. Since 2025, replays count as YouTube views but don't automatically mean engaged-view gains.
- For storytime content, OpusClip's caution suggests the "result-first" or "in medias res" hook fits better than a hard claim.

### Gaps
- No public, controlled study comparing hook types (visual vs. verbal vs. text) on organic content was found. Paddy Galloway and Retention Rabbit data were not surfaced in searches.
- No official platform statement on loop or rewatch weighting was retrieved.

## 3. Story structures that fit 15–90 seconds, and beat timing

### Takeaway
No platform prescribes narrative structures beyond hook → value/USP → CTA (TikTok official). Common structures (result-first/flashback, PAS, ABT, before-after, storytime, mini hero's journey, multi-part series) are practitioner craft. The beat timings below are [BG] guidance built around the officially supported "message within 3s, hook within 6s."

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Structure: "Have a hook, highlight your unique selling points, and end with a clear call-to-action." Source: [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [OFFICIAL, 2020] TikTok says users "still value a great story." Almost half of the top auction ads told emotionally appealing, relatable, lighthearted stories. Source: [TikTok 9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)
- [PRACTITIONER] The Vietnamese script template in circulation runs hook → main content → climax → CTA, with 3–5 variants generated for selection. Source: [FPT Shop: Prompt ChatGPT viết kịch bản](https://fptshop.com.vn/tin-tuc/for-gamers/prompt-chatgpt-viet-kich-ban-201370)
- [PRACTITIONER] Analytics framing: if retention drops mid-video, review "the pacing, build-up, and payoff." Source: [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-growth)

### Inferences
All [BG], to be validated in testing:
- **Result-first / flashback.** Show the outcome (0–3s), then "here's how I got here," then 2–3 fast beats, then a twist or lesson, then a CTA. Best for transformations and wins.
- **ABT (And–But–Therefore).** Setup ("I had X and Y"), conflict ("but Z happened"), resolution ("so I did W"). Compact and works at 20–40s.
- **PAS (Problem–Agitate–Solve).** Best for educational or advice personal brands. Risk: it sounds salesy if the "solve" is a pitch.
- **Before–after.** A visual split or transition. The hook is the contrast.
- **Storytime.** Talking head, opening in medias res with a curiosity line, escalating details, a punchline or lesson, then a loop or "part 2" prompt.
- **Mini hero's journey.** Ordinary world, call/conflict, struggle, turning point, lesson. Needs 45–90s.
- **Series / "Part 2" cliffhanger.** End on an unresolved beat. It drives profile visits and follows, but annoys viewers if overused, and each part still needs its own payoff.
- **Beat timing for 15–20s:** hook 0–2s; context 2–5s; 1–2 beats 5–14s; payoff + CTA or loop in the last 2–3s.
- **Beat timing for 30s:** hook 0–3s; setup 3–8s; rising beats 8–22s (re-hook around 10–12s); payoff 22–27s; CTA 27–30s.
- **Beat timing for 60s:** hook 0–3s; setup to ~10s; 3–4 escalating beats to ~45s with a mini re-hook every ~8–12s; climax/turn 45–55s; lesson + CTA 55–60s.
- **Beat timing for 90s:** same as 60s, plus a second act turn around 50–60s. Only worth it when the story has real escalation.
- **Rule of thumb:** one story, one point, one CTA. Put the payoff as late as retention allows, but promise it early.

### Gaps
- No platform or academic source was found that validates specific beat timings by duration. All timings above are [BG] heuristics.

## 4. Script format conventions and words per second (English and Vietnamese)

### Takeaway
Official: on-screen text at ≤5–10 words/sec (TikTok). Spoken pacing figures are [BG] for English (~2.5–3 words/sec, ≈150–180 wpm). For Vietnamese, the only figure found is a secondary citation of ~5.3 syllables/sec from cross-linguistic research. Vietnamese is a slow-syllable-rate but information-dense language, so word budgets per second should be set in syllables (âm tiết), not translated English word counts.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] On-screen text: "We recommend displaying 5-10 words per second when using text." Source: [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [STUDY via secondary] Cross-language speech-rate research (2011 study of 7 languages, 10 speakers each; 2019 follow-up of 17 languages, 170 speakers) found information rate roughly constant at ~39 bits/sec. Vietnamese has high information density (~8 bits/syllable) and a comparatively slow syllable rate. Sources: [El Gazette](https://www.elgazette.com/however-fast-humans-talk-they-deliver-the-same-amount-of-data-per-second/); [Popular Science](https://www.popsci.com/science/why-are-languages-spoken-at-different-speeds/)
- [STUDY via secondary, weak source] Vietnamese ≈5.3 syllables/sec (vs Spanish 7.7, Serbian 7.2); ≈42.5 bits/sec. The figure comes from a test-prep page reproducing the study table, so check it against the original paper. Source: [sat-questions.onrender.com](https://sat-questions.onrender.com/question/module:english-group:all-skill:all-difficulty:medium-active:all/94978129)
- [PRACTITIONER] AI script templates commonly output hook, body, and CTA, plus shot directions and on-screen-text suggestions. Source: [Jenova: Kịch bản TikTok bằng AI (May 2026)](https://www.jenova.ai/vi/resources/ai-tiktok-script-202605)

### Inferences
- **Two-column script [BG].** Left column AUDIO/VO (spoken lines, SFX, music cues); right column VISUAL (shot type, B-roll, on-screen text, transitions). Each row is one beat with a timestamp. Add a third column "TEXT OVERLAY" if overlays differ from the spoken words.
- **Spoken budgets, English [BG].** About 2.5–3 words/sec gives 15s ≈ 40–45 words, 30s ≈ 75–90, 60s ≈ 150–180, 90s ≈ 225–270. Creators often speak faster (3–3.5 w/s) with jump cuts.
- **Spoken budgets, Vietnamese.** Based on ~5.3 syllables/sec in read speech (weakly sourced), a planning range of ~4.5–5.5 âm tiết/giây seems reasonable. That gives 15s ≈ 70–80 syllables, 30s ≈ 140–160, 60s ≈ 280–320. Fast TikTok delivery may run higher. Validate by timing a read-aloud. [Inference from the study + BG]
- **Overlay text [BG].** Use far below TikTok's 5–10 w/s ceiling for key overlays: a headline hook of ≤6–8 words held 1.5–3s. Keep it inside safe zones (TikTok official). Burned-in captions help because a large share of viewers watch muted (Mosseri paraphrase, unverified).
- **B-roll [BG].** Cut to B-roll or a visual change every ~2–4s to reset attention, and use it to illustrate concrete nouns in the story.

### Gaps
- No primary Vietnamese speech-rate study was retrieved, and no Vietnamese TikTok-specific speaking-rate data was found.
- No official two-column script template from any platform was found.

## 5. Personal-brand-specific craft (storytime, vulnerability, talking head vs. faceless, authenticity, series)

### Takeaway
Official signals favour people on camera, a native or unpolished look, direct address, emotional relatable stories, original rather than reposted content, and in 2026 "Reali-TEA" authenticity. Together these give storytime talking-head content an official tailwind. Faceless formats work against "feature people" and Instagram's originality enforcement if they rely on reused footage.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] "We recommend featuring people such as creators, employees, or customers"; use a "DIY or not overly polished style." Source: [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [OFFICIAL, 2020] "Keep your story real and entertaining"; "Break the fourth wall" (direct address). Source: [TikTok 9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)
- [OFFICIAL] The TikTok Next 2026 signal "Reali-TEA" favours authenticity over escapism and real stories, humour, and emotion. Sources: [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-tiktok-next-2026-our-trend-forecast-for-marketers-for-the-year-ahead?lang=en); summary in [The Brand Berries](https://thebrandberries.com/tiktok-next-2026-trend-forecast-how-authentic-discovery-and-emotional-value-are-shaping-the-future-of-culture-and-commerce/)
- [OFFICIAL] Instagram down-ranks reposted, watermarked, and low-resolution reels, and wants "more distribution to the original content creator." Sources: [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained); [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)
- [OFFICIAL via secondary] Mosseri: "We want to be a place where a new creator with a great idea can actually reach a massive audience." Source: [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)

### Inferences
- **Vulnerability/storytime [BG].** Specific details (names, numbers, places, exact quotes) read as authentic. A lesson should be earned by a concrete moment, not stated generically. Avoid "trauma-bait" hooks that the payoff doesn't honour.
- **Consistency [BG].** A recurring series format (same opener, same title card, numbered parts) builds recognition and gives repeat viewers a reason to follow. Each episode must still stand alone, because most viewers arrive from the feed.
- **Sends [inference from Mosseri].** For non-follower growth on Instagram, design a "send" trigger, such as a line viewers would forward to someone specific ("send this to the friend who...", or simply a highly relatable moment).

### Gaps
- No controlled data comparing talking-head vs. faceless performance on organic content was found.
- No sourced data on vulnerability or storytime performance specifically.

## 6. Vietnamese context

### Takeaway
Vietnam is a TikTok-first, Facebook-dominant market. DataReportal Digital 2026 Vietnam (published 8 Nov 2025) reports TikTok ad reach of 76.1M adults (88.8% of internet users), YouTube 62.1M, Facebook 79.0M, and Instagram only 11.7M. Vietnamese-language skills should prioritise TikTok (and Facebook Reels), then Shorts, with Instagram Reels secondary.

### Cited Findings
- [STUDY] Population 102M, median age 33.4, and 85.6M internet users (84.2%) as of October 2025. Social media user identities: 79.0M (77.6%), up 7.2% YoY. Source: [DataReportal: Digital 2026 Vietnam](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam)
- [STUDY] TikTok ad reach of 76.1M (18+) equals 88.8% of internet users. That is up 6.88M (+9.9%) from end-2024 but down 4.19M (−5.2%) from July to October 2025. The audience is 53.1% male and 46.9% female. DataReportal flags the 102.9%-of-adults figure as an anomaly. Source: [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam)
- [STUDY] YouTube ad reach is 62.1M (72.5% of internet users). Facebook is 79.0M (92.3% of internet users). Instagram is 11.7M (13.6% of internet users, 56.7% female). Ad reach is not active users, and DataReportal advises against recomputing YoY changes from older reports. Source: [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam)
- [STUDY, ads tool] A TikTok Ads Manager estimate for Vietnam (2026) shows 80.6M users 18+, of whom 69.9% are aged 18–34. These are minimum audience estimates. Source: [AffectGroup](https://affectgroup.com/blog/tiktok-ads-audience-in-vietnam-2026-breakdown/)
- [PRACTITIONER, Vietnamese] Local guides repeat the 3-second rule ("chỉ cần 3 giây đầu không đủ cuốn hút, người xem sẽ lướt qua ngay") and the hook → nội dung → cao trào → CTA template. Source: [FPT Shop](https://fptshop.com.vn/tin-tuc/for-gamers/prompt-chatgpt-viet-kich-ban-201370)

### Inferences
- The 18–34 skew and the TikTok-first reach suggest Vietnamese scripts should default to TikTok-native conventions: fast spoken Vietnamese, burned-in captions, and trend audio. Re-export clean, watermark-free files for Reels and Shorts.

## 7. Common mistakes that kill retention

### Takeaway
Two kinds of failure are supported by platform evidence: a slow start (key message not in the first 3s) and non-native or recycled production (watermarks, reposts, low resolution, horizontal, mostly text, muted). Everything else in this section is practitioner or [BG] craft knowledge.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Instagram makes these less visible: low-resolution or watermarked reels; muted reels; reels with borders; reels that are mostly text; reels already posted on Instagram. Source: [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL/STUDY 2020] Burying the key message hurts: top-CTR TikTok videos put it in the first 3s. Vertical 9:16 averages a 25% higher 6-second watch-through rate. Source: [TikTok 9 creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/9-creative-tips-to-drive-auction-ad-performance)
- [OFFICIAL] TikTok's guidance covers overly polished, non-native creative ("DIY or not overly polished style"), content outside the UI safe zone, and overlay text faster than 5–10 words/sec. It also says to refresh creatives when performance declines. Source: [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en)
- [PRACTITIONER] A weak hook shows up as low stayed-to-watch. Slow pacing or a missing payoff shows up as low average % viewed after a good start. Source: [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-growth)

### Inferences
Mistakes for a scoring rubric, all [BG] unless tagged otherwise:
1. Throat-clearing intros ("Hi guys, today I want to...", "Xin chào mọi người, hôm nay...").
2. Logo or title card first.
3. Hook promises something the video doesn't pay off, which costs trust and completion.
4. Multiple stories or points in one video.
5. Context dumps before the conflict.
6. Static shot longer than 4–5s with no visual change.
7. Payoff revealed too early, so nothing pulls viewers to the end.
8. CTA too long or too early.
9. No captions (muted viewers).
10. Text in the UI-covered zones [OFFICIAL safe-zone guidance].
11. Recycled footage with watermarks [OFFICIAL Instagram].
12. Generic, unspecific vulnerability.
13. Overuse of "Part 2" bait without resolution.
14. Music drowning out the voice.
15. Length padded beyond the story's natural length.
